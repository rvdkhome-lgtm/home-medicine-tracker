Expiry reminders *

Enter one reminder offset per row: a negative number means days before expiry; 0 means on expiry day; a positive number means days after expiry.

Default reminders: 2 days before, 1 day before, on expiry day, and 1 day after. Remove or change these as needed.

APP SETTINGS

Settings

Google Drive sync

Use the same Google account on every family device. This app creates a single inventory JSON file in the signed-in Google Drive.

Notifications

Notifications can be checked when this app is open. Allow notifications in Android/Chrome settings. A browser may pause checks while the app is closed.

Data

Inventory is saved on this device and can be exported as a JSON backup. Drive sync is recommended before switching devices or clearing browser data.

ALERTS

Reminder settings

Each medicine has its own list of reminder offsets. For example, -2, -1, 0, 1 triggers two days before, one day before, on the expiry date and one day after.

Notifications are generated on this device. Each family member can enable notifications on their own phone after opening the shared inventory.
